Credit every reference by creator name and link. Borrow grammar (rhythm, structure, transition type, camera idea). Never borrow assets (footage, music, characters, logos, copy). Full policy: `05_RESEARCH_NOTES/technique-breakdowns/reference-credit-policy.md`.
