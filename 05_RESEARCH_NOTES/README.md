# 05 Research Notes

The living part of the knowledge base. Sections 01–04 hold rules. This section holds the evidence and the experiments that change those rules.

| Folder | What goes here |
|---|---|
| `technique-breakdowns/` | Analysis of real videos and kits: what they do, measured, with credit |
| `workflow-experiments/` | Tests of a method, with a hypothesis and a result |
| `lessons-learned/` | Notes from real projects, and the loop that turns notes into rules |

## What belongs here and what does not

- **Real work only.** A note records something that happened: a measurement, a test, a mistake. Do not generate "lessons" from imagination. They mislead the system later.
- **Seeded entries** from the reference kits are clearly labelled external. Replace them with your own evidence as you collect it.
- **Promote or retire.** When a note proves out three times, promote it to a rule in sections 01–04 (and link back). When it fails, mark it `retired` and say why.

## Status labels for notes

`idea` → `tested-once` → `confirmed` → `promoted` (rule written) or `retired`
