## Suggested first experiments

These are ideas, not results. Run them on real client footage, one at a time.

1. **Cut style:** cut only between phrases vs also removing fillers. Compare naturalness and length.
2. **Caption chunk size:** 1–2 words vs 2–3 words. Compare how easy they are to read.
3. **Hook length:** hook line under 3 s vs 5 s. Compare early drop-off.
4. **B-roll mix:** more screen recording vs more AI video. Compare watch time and cost.
5. **Music level:** 9 dB vs 14 dB under voice. Compare clarity on a phone speaker.
6. **Render path:** native 1080p vs 4K downscaled. Compare edge sharpness and file size.
7. **Transcription tool:** two tools on the same clip. Compare word errors and timestamp accuracy.
8. **Opening frame:** split with brand on top vs full-face. Compare early retention.
9. **Tone sentence:** with and without a written tone line. Compare how many rework rounds each needs.

## Rules for fair tests

- Change one thing at a time.
- Same footage, same length.
- Judge by numbers first, then people.
- One result is a hint. Repeat before you promote a rule.
