# Transition atlas

A transition should say something: *this leads to that*, *time passed*, *we are zooming in*. If it says nothing, use a hard cut. Every entry lists what it is good for and what to avoid pairing it with.

## Cuts that carry meaning

| Transition | What it does | Fits tones | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hard cut on action** | Cuts while something moves so motion carries across | All | Cutting on a static frame with no reason |
| **Beat cut** | Cut lands on a music hit or spoken stress | Urgent, bold, celebratory | Slow, calm films |
| **Match cut (shape)** | A circle becomes a coin, a line becomes a horizon | Trustworthy, premium, clever | Two shapes that only vaguely match |
| **Graphic match (colour)** | A colour flood bridges two scenes | Bold, playful | More than two accent colours in one shot |
| **Object carry** | One object travels into the next scene and becomes something else | Playful, celebratory, warm | Heavy realism |
| **Type carry** | A word is carried across the cut and reused | Trustworthy, bold | Long sentences |
| **Cursor carry** | A cursor leads the eye into the next screen | Technical, product demos | Non-software films |

## Camera-driven

| Transition | What it does | Fits tones | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Whip pan** | Fast pan with blur; the cut hides in the smear | Urgent, bold, energetic | Calm and premium |
| **Push-through** | Camera moves through a surface into the next scene | Premium, mysterious | Fast urgent pacing |
| **Scale-through type** | Camera dollies through a giant word | Bold, premium | Small text |
| **Dolly-out reveal** | Pull back to reveal the bigger picture | Celebratory, proof moments | Beginnings of films |
| **Rack focus** | Focus shifts from one plane to another | Premium, warm | Flat 2D layouts |
| **Speed ramp** | Slow → fast → slow around the cut | Urgent, sporty | Calm tones |

## Masks and materials

| Transition | What it does | Fits tones | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mask wipe** | A shape or edge reveals the next scene | Trustworthy, technical | Random decorative shapes |
| **Text mask** | Next scene plays inside letters | Bold, mysterious | Thin fonts |
| **Focal iris** | A circle opens or closes on a subject | Calm, mysterious, nostalgic | High-energy cuts |
| **Glass refraction** | Scene bends through a glass shape | Premium | Cheap-looking glass presets |
| **Paper fold / ink bleed** | Physical materials | Warm, human, nostalgic | Technical tones |
| **Dither / pixel sort** | Digital break-up | Technical, nostalgic | Warm, premium |
| **Film burn** | Light-leak flash | Nostalgic, celebratory | Clean corporate |
| **Flash through white** | Act break | Bold, celebratory | Used more than once or twice |

## Time and structure

| Transition | What it does | Fits tones | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Time-lapse tick** | Clock or sky speeds up to show time passing | Celebratory, trustworthy | Over-long |
| **Freeze and rewind** | Stop, then reverse to explain what just happened | Mysterious, playful | Serious problem statements |
| **Split stack** | Panels slide in to compare | Technical, trustworthy | Too many panels |
| **Recolour without cutting** | Same layout, colours change, meaning shifts | Related ideas | Unrelated scenes |
| **Line to horizon** | A line becomes the horizon of the next scene | Trustworthy, premium | Busy scenes |

## Default whip (energetic films)

Vertical whip with matched velocity. Outgoing scene moves up about 150 px with 30 px blur in 0.33 s on an ease-in. Incoming scene starts 150 px below with the same blur and settles over about 1 s on an ease-out. Same direction on both sides, so velocity matches at the cut.

## Rules

1. Pick the transition from the story beat, then check it fits the tone.
2. Do not repeat the same transition inside one film unless it is a deliberate motif.
3. Do not use a whoosh sound on every cut. Sound goes with real spatial moves.
4. Hide the swap inside the moment of most blur or brightness.
5. If you cannot say what the transition means, use a hard cut.
