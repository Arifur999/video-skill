# Titles and lower thirds

## Titles

- Say one thing. A title is a label, not a paragraph.
- Hold long enough to read plus a beat: roughly 0.3 s per word plus 0.5–1 s.
- Do not open with a title card in short-form. Start with the hook.
- Section cards in a list reel: a short numbered or named card, then the visuals.
- Keep text away from edges. Leave a safe margin (about 5–10% of the frame) in landscape and respect platform zones in vertical.

## Lower thirds (name and role)

- Appear a moment after the person starts speaking, not on the first frame.
- Two lines at most: name (larger), role (smaller).
- On screen about 4–6 seconds, then out.
- Animate in simply (slide plus fade, 0.3–0.5 s). Out slightly faster.
- Place so it does not cover the mouth. Lower left is conventional in landscape.
- One style for the whole video.

## Hierarchy

Size, weight, and colour set rank. The key word should be readable in half a second. Supporting text can be half the size. Labels should never compete with the headline.

## Consistency kit (write it down once per project)

- Primary font and weight
- Accent font (if any)
- Colours and what each means
- Entrance and exit style
- Safe margins
- Box or no box

## Common mistakes

- Titles shown for too short a time.
- Too many words.
- Different animation on every title (unless the film is deliberately varied; see the variety rules).
- Low contrast over footage.
- Text on top of faces.
- Placing text where platform UI will cover it.
