# Short-form playbook

> **Read this as one creator's measured style**, taken from frame-by-frame study of his own top-performing reels. Use the numbers as a starting point. Re-measure with your own best content as you collect it (see `05_RESEARCH_NOTES`).

## Pacing numbers

- Speech starts within about 0.1 s. The file ends right after the last word. No intro or outro card.
- Pauses between phrases stay under about 0.2 s. Natural beats after a comma or list marker run 0.2–0.4 s and stay.
- Typical speaking rate in the studied reels: roughly 4.0–4.7 words per second. Tightness comes from removing dead air, not speeding up.
- Shots average about 1.1–1.4 s, none longer than about 2.8 s.
- First cut lands at about 0.6–1.0 s, on the first charged word.
- Each item in a spoken list gets its own shot.

## Layout mix (vertical)

| Layout | Share of the reel |
|---|---|
| Split (B-roll top, speaker bottom) | about 30–42% |
| Full B-roll | about 50–55% |
| Punch-in on the speaker | about 5–15% |

- Speaker on screen about 45% of the time; never absent for more than about 3.5 s.
- First frame: a split with the topic's tool or brand on top.
- Every layout change is a hard cut.

## Story shape

Hook (first 3 s) → one line of credibility → open loop (blurred list) → items → payoff that calls back → CTA (show the resource, then a punch-in with the comment keyword).

## Format variants

**YouTube Short cut from a long video:** screen recording on top, face cam below, word-highlight captions on the seam. Value or claim in the first 3 s, then proofs in the long video's order, ending on a complete takeaway. Keep labels below the top safe margin. Never assemble a CTA the speaker did not say.

**Narrated explainer (synthetic voice):** put the whole timeline on one beat grid; level every voice line to the same loudness (limit only the peaks); re-transcribe the full narration and compare it to the script before every render; do not play synthetic narration over footage of a real person talking; end on the thesis line, then the logo.

## Anti-patterns

- Intro cards and logos before the hook.
- Long setup before the claim.
- A caption style that changes mid-video.
- Stock footage that does not match the noun.
- Sound effects on every cut.
- Ending with a fade-out on the last word.

## Platform safe zones

Platform UI covers parts of the frame (usernames, captions, buttons). Keep key text and faces out of the top and bottom bands. Exact pixel margins change as apps update, so keep a current figure in `04_SOFTWARE_MANUALS/` or the delivery specs, and verify before each project. Treat any specific number as `verify-first`.
