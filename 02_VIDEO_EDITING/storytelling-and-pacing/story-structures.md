# Story structures

Pick one structure per video. Write it down before any visuals.

## 1. Hook → Proof → Items → Payoff → CTA (explainer / list reel)

1. **Hook (0–3 s):** a claim or command that names the person, tool, or problem.
2. **Credibility (1 line):** why listen.
3. **Open loop:** if there is a list, show all items early (blurred or small) so the viewer waits to see them.
4. **Items:** each gets a title, a one-line definition, two or three literal visuals, and a punch-in on the key line.
5. **Payoff:** call back to the hook visual.
6. **CTA:** show the resource, then say what to do.

## 2. Hook → Reveal → Proof → CTA (product promo)

The four-beat shape used across the saas-motion-kit themes.

- **Hook:** the pain or a surprising image.
- **Reveal:** the product appears and does one clear thing.
- **Proof moment:** one number, one result, or one demo that earns trust.
- **CTA:** one action, held long enough to read.

## 3. Problem → Brand → Benefits → Surfaces → Products → Foundation → CTA → Silence

Three-act brand spot. Use threes: three benefits, three surfaces, three product names. End on a short silence so the last line lands.

## 4. Before / After

Show the old way briefly, cut hard to the new way, hold on the result. Works in 15 seconds.

## 5. Question → Answer

Ask the question the viewer has. Give a short answer. Offer one deeper step.

## Principles that apply to all

- **Truthful hook and payoff.** Preserve the speaker's meaning. Never invent a result.
- **Specific beats generic.** A named number beats "big results".
- **Open loops.** Make the viewer want the next thing.
- **Callbacks.** Return to one earlier image or line.
- **Resolve.** End with a complete takeaway, not a trailing thought.
- **One idea per beat.** Two ideas means two beats.
- **Name things before you use them.** Do not show a number or tool the viewer was never introduced to.
- **Every number on screen must be explainable** from what the video showed.

## Story checklist

- [ ] Can I say the message in one sentence?
- [ ] Does the first 3 seconds make a specific promise?
- [ ] Is there a moment of proof?
- [ ] Does the ending call back to the opening?
- [ ] Is there exactly one call to action?
