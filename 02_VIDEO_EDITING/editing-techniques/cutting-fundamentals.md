# Cutting fundamentals

## Why you cut

Cut for one of these reasons: new information, new angle, new emotion, new energy, or to remove dead time. If a cut does none, remove it.

## Core cut types

| Cut | What | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Hard cut** | Straight change | Default; fast, honest |
| **Cut on action** | Cut mid-movement so the motion hides the edit | Any physical move, gesture, or camera move |
| **J-cut** | Next scene's audio starts before its picture | Smooth entry into a new scene; dialogue continuity |
| **L-cut** | Previous scene's audio continues over the next picture | Reaction shots; keeping a voice going across B-roll |
| **Match cut** | Shape, movement, or idea matches across the cut | Clever links between scenes |
| **Jump cut** | Same angle, time removed | Talking-head pacing; the norm in short-form |
| **Smash cut** | Abrupt shift in volume or mood | Comedy, shock; use rarely |
| **Cutaway** | Insert related footage | Covers a cut, adds evidence |
| **Punch-in** | Digital zoom on the same footage | Emphasis; hides a jump cut |

## Rhythm

- Cut length sets energy. Short = urgent. Long = calm.
- Vary lengths. A steady metronome numbs the viewer.
- Cut slightly **before** the beat or action peak, not after.
- Hold a shot a moment longer for a payoff, then cut.
- Build a pattern, then break it once.

## Continuity

- Keep screen direction consistent (a subject moving left to right stays that way).
- Keep eyelines consistent.
- Match brightness, colour, and sound level across a cut.
- Do not cut between two nearly identical framings; change the size or angle clearly (about 30% difference at least).

## Sound leads picture

Many cuts feel right only because of the audio. Place the audio edit first, then match picture. Cut audio between phrases, never inside a word.

## Edit passes (in this order)

1. **Assembly:** all usable material in order.
2. **Rough cut:** story works, ignoring polish.
3. **Fine cut:** trims, rhythm, transitions.
4. **Picture lock:** no more story changes.
5. **Finish:** captions, grade, sound mix, final graphics.
6. **QC and deliver.**

Do not polish a section before the story works. Do not change the story after picture lock without restarting the finish passes.
